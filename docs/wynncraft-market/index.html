Price definitions, coverage & sources

The default price is Wynnventory’s average of daily average listing prices over the displayed date window. The trimmed option averages its daily middle-80% prices. Neither is a transaction-weighted sale price or an exact-roll appraisal.

Observations are summed daily listing counts: the same unsold listing may appear on several days. The smaller daily figure is the mean count on days represented in the archive. Low/high are observed extremes over the window. Non-shiny items only.

All received market rows and official item records are retained. Exact display or internal names are joined, with item types checked. Duplicate item variants share a name-based price and are labelled. Unmatched market items have unknown levels and sources. Items without price data stay visible with “No price”. Filters and pagination always show counts.

Level means a combat requirement for equipment, crafting level for ingredients, and gathering level for materials/tools. An item’s level does not establish the level or quests needed to obtain it. Known quest and trade restrictions are shown in its row.

Acquisition comes from official item metadata, supplemented by linked wiki sources. “Excluded from ordinary drops” does not mean unobtainable. Unresolved special, legacy or seasonal sources remain explicit. This is a dated snapshot; it does not update itself while you browse.
